Alice's story

Hello, I'm Alice. I have signed up to go on a 14-day expedition to Borneo in July 2029. It is more than just a trip: it will involve trekking, supporting community projects, cultural visits and outdoor pursuits.

Over the next two years I will also take part in teamwork and leadership training, and practise hiking and other outdoor pursuits.

I have set up Cahaya to raise the money myself, selling candles and other homemade gifts. I chose Cahaya because it is the Malay word for light.

I make every candle at home with help from my mum: weighing the wax, adding the scent, pouring, labelling and packing.

Looking after your candle

  • Trim the wick to 5mm before each burn.
  • Let the wax melt right to the edge of the tin the first time you light it.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep it away from children, pets and draughts.
  • The tin gets hot. Stand it on a heatproof surface.

Safety information

Every candle carries a safety label on its base and the allergen and hazard wording for its scent on the back. If you have allergies or questions about a scent, email us before you buy.
